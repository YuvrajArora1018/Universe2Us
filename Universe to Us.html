About

Universe to Us believes every child deserves to understand their place in the cosmos. Launched in October 2024, it bridges educational gaps by introducing young minds to the fundamentals of existence, and builds confidence and critical thinking along the way.

How we teach

Immersive, story-driven sessions with real-life examples, hands-on activities and open dialogue. Complex ideas become experiences students can relate to.

The idea

It began on a school night, when curiosity about our place in the cosmos turned into a first proposal by 2 a.m. A whole team took it from there.

V1 and beyond

On 9 January 2026 the Founding Board, then finishing Grade 12, became the Advisory Council. Board duties are now compartmentalised across another set of enthusiastic students who carry the mission forward.

The team

Our six founding members, introduced in October 2024 and now our Advisory Council, and Nathan Fernandes, who sits on the current board.
